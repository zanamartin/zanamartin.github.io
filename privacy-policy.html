Thank you for visiting www.zanamartin.com. Your privacy is important to me. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected through this website and how it is used.

Information I Collect

This website only collects personal information that you voluntarily provide. If you subscribe to my newsletter, I collect the information you submit, such as your email address.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to send occasional newsletters and updates about my books and writing. I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

Newsletter

My newsletter is managed using MailerLite. When you subscribe, your information is stored and processed by MailerLite so that I can send email updates. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You may unsubscribe at any time, and you will no longer receive future emails.

This website does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. Basic technical information, such as server logs, may be collected automatically by my web hosting provider to help operate and secure the website.

Third-Party Services

This website uses MailerLite to manage newsletter subscriptions. Information you provide when subscribing is processed according to MailerLite's privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
