Join My ARC Team

Love reading? Help launch my next book.

Reviews are the lifeblood of indie authors. They help new readers discover a book, and for an independent writer like me, they can make all the difference. That’s why I’m building a team of readers who get early access to my upcoming releases, and I’d love for you to be part of it.

What is an ARC team?

ARC stands for Advance Reader Copy. As a member of my ARC team, you’ll receive free early copies of my new books before they’re available to the public, in exchange for an honest review.

What ARC readers do

  • Receive a free advance copy of each new release in ebook or PDF format
  • Read it before launch day
  • Post an honest review on Amazon and/or Goodreads by or shortly after release day
  • Optionally, share the book on social media

How to join

1. Click the link below.

2. Fill out the short Google Form.

3. I’ll add you to the team and email you whenever a new ARC is ready.
